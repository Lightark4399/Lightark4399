### Projects

- **[backtest-audit](https://github.com/Lightark4399/backtest-audit)** —
  A Python toolkit that tests whether a backtest's headline performance reflects
  genuine predictive skill. Its demo shows a zero-skill predictor scoring an
  annualised Sharpe of 147 that falls to −4.5 once the entity level is removed
  from the labels.
- **[factor-zoo-audit](https://github.com/Lightark4399/factor-zoo-audit)** —
  A point-in-time factor research pipeline on SEC XBRL filings that records its own
  evidence limits. On a selected 200-company sample, a naive fundamentals query could
  see at least one later-filed record on each of 184 of 184 checked ROE signal dates.
